### Rule #25 — revised draft (496 characters, awaiting approval)

```
Kau pilih lot paling kecil, 0.01. Rasa dah cukup selamat.

Tapi lot kecil tak semestinya risk kecil. Risk ikut jarak SL.

0.01 lot, SL 40 pips: kena sekali, -$4.
0.01 lot, SL 80 pips: kena sekali, -$8.

Rule #25: kira risk dalam $ sebelum entry. Kalau lot paling kecil pun dah lebih dari risk yang kau tetapkan untuk satu trade, skip setup tu. Jangan kecilkan SL semata-mata supaya muat.

Lot paling kecil belum tentu cukup kecil.

Sebelum entry, kau kira risk dulu, atau baru tahu lepas kena SL?
```

What changed, and why it fits the published method:

- Removed "Lot ikut formula 1%" (the owner's system has no universal 1% rule) and the cent-account advice.
- Removed every account-size figure ($100, $500), so no deposit threshold is stated or implied.
- Kept the lesson of the original ("Lot paling kecil belum tentu cukup kecil") and expressed risk in dollars at a given stop. This follows the published Rules #9 (lot follows the stop), #10 (risk = lot size × stop distance) and #15 (stop goes where the idea is wrong).
- Pip values use the convention published in Rule #9 (1 lot = 100 oz, 1 pip = $0.10): 0.01 lot × 40 pips = $4; × 80 pips = $8.
