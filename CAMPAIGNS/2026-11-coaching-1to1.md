## 3. Claims: what the posts say and do not say

The owner's brief was: the system is proven, and with capital as low as $150 a trader can survive and will not need to deposit again.

| Brief | Used in posts as | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| "Proven" | Not used yet | Needs real numbers or real, consented feedback (question 4). No invented testimonials. |
| "Dengan $150 dah boleh survive" | "$150: modal minimum yang aku syorkan untuk mula", with the reason, and a line that $150 is not safe from loss | The owner's recommendation is his to make. "Safe" on its own could be read as protection from loss. |
| "Takkan depo-depo dah" | "supaya kau tak perlu top-up sebab blow" as the aim of what is taught, next to "Aku tak janji untung" | A promise that someone will never deposit again is an outcome guarantee. The account's own post of 1 Nov says it will never promise profit. |

## 5. Disclosure (required if coaching is tied to a broker account)

If the coaching is free for people who open an account through the owner, the post or the first WhatsApp reply must say so plainly. Suggested wording:

```
Full disclosure: aku Introducing Broker. Kalau kau buka akaun trading melalui aku, broker bayar aku bila kau trade. Coaching ni sebahagian dari servis aku untuk client.
```

Suggested `[SYARAT]` lines:

- Tied to an account: `Free untuk client yang buka akaun melalui aku. Aku IB, broker bayar aku bila kau trade.`
- Paid: `RM__ satu sesi.`
- Free for anyone: `Free. Takde syarat buka akaun.`

Note: `BRAND/brand-identity.md` section 8.1 says education is never gated behind the referral. The free daily rules, PDF and webinar satisfy that. One-to-one coaching as a client service is a separate commercial offer; if the owner confirms that model, that line in the policy should be reworded to say so.

## 7. Limits to know about

- **Threads is not local.** Posts reach followers across Malaysia; only a fraction will be in the owner's area. Naming the area in the first lines helps the right people self-select, but local reach is better served by referrals from past coaching students and the owner's own WhatsApp contacts and status.
- **Capacity.** One-to-one sessions cap how many people can be served per week. If demand exceeds that, small group sessions (3–5 people) in the same area keep the face-to-face format.
- **Post D describes a session flow that the drafter proposed.** Replace it with what actually happens in the owner's sessions.
