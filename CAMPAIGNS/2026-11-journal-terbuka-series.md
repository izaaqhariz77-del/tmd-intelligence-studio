## Why

The owner wants results content (21 Nov). His 21 Nov post about a $150 starting balance drew 196 views in its first 40 minutes, faster than any campaign post that week. That is one data point, not proof. Results have pull.

The same post carried what the campaign warns against: a balance screenshot, a daily-income figure and a capital figure, with no disclaimer. This format keeps the pull of real results and drops those parts. TMD shows the full record, losses included, in R. That is what the campaign already teaches (Rules #14, #29, #32, #33 and #36).

The owner decided to keep the 21 Nov post.

## Format rules (proposed; the owner approves before use)

1. **The full week, every trade.** Wins and losses. No picking the best trades.
2. **Results in R only.** No RM, $, %, balance, equity, starting capital, daily income or salary comparisons.
3. **Each trade labelled** "ikut plan" or "langgar rule", naming the rule (Rule #33).
4. **Screenshots:** the journal, and charts before entry and after close (Rule #32). Never the balance, account number, name, broker name or logo, or deposit and withdrawal lines.
5. **Real trades from the owner only.** Nothing estimated or rounded up. The assistant checks the R figures before posting.
6. **Before the disclosure:** "Result lepas bukan jaminan untuk minggu depan." The standard disclosure stays the last line.
7. **No broker, account or coaching call to action.** The free community invitation is optional.
8. **A week where risk per trade broke the owner's own rules** is either not used or says so plainly.

## Timing

- Created on Saturday morning from the owner's data, never scheduled in advance. The weekly batch keeps a one-line question in the Saturday 12:30pm slot as the fallback.
- If the data has not arrived by Saturday 10:00am, the question post goes out instead.

## What the owner sends each week

| Item | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Every trade Mon–Fri: date and session, buy or sell, entry, SL, TP, result in R, and whether it followed the plan | The numbers in the post | Journal screenshot or typed list. If results are in pips or $, include the risk per trade so the assistant can convert to R. |
| Two charts, one win and one loss, before entry and after close | Images for the post | Zone, SL and TP marked |
| MT5 trade history for the week (optional) | Checking only; never posted, never stored in this repository | Crop away the account number, name, broker and balance |

Screenshots stay out of this repository; it is public. They are uploaded to Zernio only when the post is scheduled.
