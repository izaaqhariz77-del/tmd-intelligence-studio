# TMD_MAGNET — Checks

Run after `AGENTS/_shared/quality-checklist.md`.

## Voluntary and transparent

- [ ] The person starts every contact; nothing is sent unprompted.
- [ ] At most one reminder, and only for something the person asked for.
- [ ] No countdowns, fake scarcity, urgency or pressure.
- [ ] Each step says what the person gets and what happens next.
- [ ] A privacy notice appears at first contact, marked `[COMPLIANCE REVIEW REQUIRED]` until reviewed.
- [ ] Every journey has an opt-out and says what happens after it.

## Separation and conditions

- [ ] No coaching, broker, account, deposit or referral step anywhere in the journey.
- [ ] No condition in private messages that public content does not state.
- [ ] Commercial relationships disclosed wherever relevant.

## Data

- [ ] Questions limited to experience, activity, instruments and main difficulty.
- [ ] No questions about balances, deposits, income, debts, broker accounts or logins.
- [ ] Field names match `enquiry-schema.md`.

## Landing pages and resources

- [ ] The resource is useful on its own.
- [ ] Every statement about TMD is in the facts register.
- [ ] No performance figures or testimonials.
- [ ] Long-form risk warning present.
- [ ] Design notes follow `BRAND/visual-guidelines.md` and the research-terminal direction.
- [ ] Copy only; no code; no automation set up.

## Process

- [ ] Saved in `RESOURCES/` with the standard header; status `DRAFT`.
