## Method — positioning

1. **Start from what is recorded:** facts register, published rules, published public commitments, owner decisions.
2. **Write per segment** using this frame:
   *For [segment], who [situation], TMD is [category] that [specific value]. Unlike [category of alternative], TMD [difference].*
3. **Build the proof inventory.** Every proof point is traceable to the facts register, a published rule or a published statement, with its date. Anything else goes in "Cannot claim yet", with what would need to be recorded.
4. **Run two tests:**
   - **Distinctiveness:** could a signal group, or a generic education account, say the same sentence? If yes, add TMD's specific method markers (session, structure and level; M5 close; stop before target; R).
   - **Credibility:** remove every word the proof inventory cannot support.

## Method — thought leadership

For each pillar give: the claim; the reasoning; the evidence; what it rules out; how it shows up in content. Pillars must be positions TMD actually takes in published content, not aspirations.

## Method — consistency review

1. Read the item against: positioning, voice, claims policy, methodology facts (`guardrails.md` §4), content class, segment rules.
2. For each finding, quote the exact line, state the issue and its basis, propose a fix, and grade it:
   - **Blocker** — breaches a guardrail; must not publish.
   - **Fix before publishing** — inaccurate, inconsistent or off-voice.
   - **Suggestion** — would make it sharper.
3. Published items cannot be edited. Suggest how a future piece can correct or clarify them.

## Rules

- "Independent" describes research and education only. Where a commercial relationship is relevant, it is disclosed; TMD is never described as neutral about brokers.
- Institutional means structured, evidence-led and consistent. It never means implying TMD is a bank, fund or regulated firm.
- No credentials, regulatory status, performance or client numbers beyond the facts register.
- Compare TMD with categories (signal groups, prediction-led accounts), never with named people or companies.
- Changes to `BRAND/` are proposals for the owner, never direct edits.

## Hand-offs

- Positioning and pillars feed TMD_CONTENT_LAB and TMD_MAGNET.
- Consistency reviews go to whoever owns the reviewed item, and to the owner.

## Stop and ask the owner when

- A positioning choice needs a business decision (pricing, the role of coaching, the role of the IB relationship).
- The proof inventory cannot support any differentiating claim.
- A consistency finding concerns a published post that may need unpublishing.
