## 1. Positioning statement (draft)

> For experienced gold traders who already understand leverage and drawdown, The Market Doctrine is independent market research and trading education that works from conditions, not predictions: session, structure and level before any entry, the stop decided before the target, and results measured in R. Unlike signal groups and prediction-led accounts, TMD shows its reasoning and what would prove it wrong. It does not sell hope.

## 2. Tests

- **Distinctiveness.** "Conditions, not predictions" alone is generic; many education accounts say it. TMD's specific method markers (Rule #1's three conditions, the stop before the target, R measurement) carry the difference, so they stay in the statement.
- **Credibility.** Removed from an earlier draft: "institutional-grade" (implies a firm TMD is not) and "proven" (no track record is recorded).

## 3. Thought-leadership pillars

| Pillar | Claim | What it rules out | How it shows up |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conditions over predictions | A trade needs three conditions: session, structure, level. Without them there is no trade. | Price targets presented as certainty; "confirm naik" | Scenario briefs with invalidation; no-trade days treated as decisions (queued Rule #27) |
| Execution is measurable | Process is judged in R and over a sample, not by one trade or a screenshot. | P&L screenshots; strategy-hopping after three losses | Rules #14 and #26; weekly journal review (queued Rule #29) |
| Costs and conditions are part of the edge | Spread, rollover and news windows change the trade before analysis does. | Entering at rollover or into a release | Rules #7 and #8; pre-release briefings such as 25 Nov |

The third pillar is where PRIMARY interest in "execution quality and trading infrastructure" belongs. TMD teaches the costs and conditions. It does not name, rate or recommend any provider (`guardrails.md` §7).
