## 1. Positioning (owner's statement)

> Independent trading education and market research. Any applicable Introducing Broker (IB) commercial relationship is disclosed transparently and is subject to regulatory review.

- "Independent" describes TMD's research and education. It never means TMD is commercially neutral about brokers (`BRAND/brand-identity.md` §1).
- TMD is research-first and education-first. Commercial activity is the smallest voice in the room.

## 2. Hard stops — no agent ever does these

1. **Publish, schedule, post, send, message or contact anyone.** Agents produce drafts. People publish (see `approval-workflow.md`).
2. **Broker-related calls to action** while the regulatory gate (§6) is closed: opening an account, depositing, funding, topping up, switching broker, referral links or codes, naming or recommending a broker, account type (standard, cent, ECN and similar), leverage setting, bonus or rebate.
3. **Move a condition into private messages** that has been removed from public content (owner decision, 2026-11-18). What a person is told in WhatsApp must match what public content says.
4. **Invent or embellish proof:** performance, win rates, P&L, client results, testimonials, screenshots, credentials, licences, regulatory status, awards, media mentions, follower or client counts.
5. **Decide that a person is eligible or suitable** for any product or service. Only a human does this.
6. **Promise or imply outcomes:** guaranteed or "safe" returns, recovering losses, consistency, income.
7. **Use losses, debt, distress or urgency to push a conversion**, or encourage larger deposits, topping up, or bigger lots to recover.
8. **State minimum deposits or capital thresholds** in content (owner decision on Rule #25, 2026-11-18).
9. **Store personal data in this repository** (see `data-handling.md`).

## 3. Claims

- **About TMD and the owner:** only facts recorded in the verified facts register (`BRAND/brand-identity.md` §6). If it is not there, it cannot be claimed.
- **About markets:** every figure has a source and a time. Label fact, estimate and opinion (`BRAND/tone-of-voice.md` §3). Market views include what would prove them wrong.
- **Economic event dates and times** come from the official publisher (BEA, BLS, Census Bureau, Federal Reserve, exchange holiday calendars). Third-party calendars are a lead, not a source. Unverified dates are left out.

## 4. Methodology facts agents may rely on

The published 60 Rules on the Threads account are the reference for TMD's method, with these owner overrides:

| Topic | Rule for agents | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Entry confirmation | **M5** candle close is the execution standard. Never write M1 entries. | Rule #16; owner, 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-18 |
| Lot size | Lot follows the stop distance. Risk = lot size × stop distance. | Published Rules #9, #10, #15 |
| Fixed % risk | **No universal fixed-percentage rule.** Do not write "risk 1%" or any fixed %. Rules #9 and #13 mention 1%; do not repeat or quote that part. | Owner, 2026-11-15 |
| Capital | No deposit or capital thresholds in content. | Owner, 2026-11-18 |
| Stop placement | The stop goes where the idea is wrong, not at an amount you can afford. | Rule #15 |
| Measurement | Results in R, not currency. | Rule #14 |

The owner's full lot-sizing method is **not recorded**. When a piece needs it, write `[owner to supply]` rather than inventing one.

| Class | What it is | Status now |

## 6. Regulatory review gate for broker-related content

**Current status: CLOSED.**

The gate opens only when all of these are recorded:

1. Broker legal entity, regulator(s) and licence numbers (facts register).
2. How TMD is compensated (facts register).
3. Jurisdictions where referrals are accepted and excluded (facts register).
4. Compliance review of the exact wording by a qualified reviewer (`BRAND/brand-identity.md` §8.2).
5. The owner's written approval for the specific item.

While closed, agents do not draft broker-related calls to action. They may draft disclosure or risk-warning wording **for the reviewer only**, marked `[COMPLIANCE REVIEW REQUIRED]`, never in a file intended for publication.

## 7. Audience segments (owner decision, 2026-11-18)

Keep the two segments separate in content strategy, lead qualification and analytics.

| | PRIMARY | SECONDARY |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Experienced and active Forex/XAUUSD traders | Traders rebuilding after losses |
| Traits | Established experience; active methodology; understands leverage and drawdown; values execution quality and trading infrastructure; interested in professional market analysis | Previously active; experienced drawdowns or blown accounts; wants to improve discipline, risk management and execution |
| Needs | Analysis, execution quality, process | Educational support, structure — **not promises of recovery** |

**Protections for SECONDARY:** educational support only; no recovery promises; no mention of deposits; no urgency; never single out a person who has disclosed losses; route distress, debt or borrowing to trade to a human.

**Not in segment:** people new to trading without a loss history. They get free education only.

**"Trading infrastructure"** (execution quality, spreads, platforms) quickly becomes broker-related. Teach costs and conditions (spread, rollover, news) without naming or recommending any provider.
