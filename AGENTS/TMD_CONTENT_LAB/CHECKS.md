# TMD_CONTENT_LAB — Checks

Run after `AGENTS/_shared/quality-checklist.md`.

## Platform

- [ ] Threads: every post is 500 characters or fewer, disclaimer included; exact count reported.
- [ ] Instagram: 5–8 slides; caption 2,200 characters or fewer; 5 hashtags or fewer.

## Method and accuracy

- [ ] Entry confirmation is M5. No M1 entries.
- [ ] Lot follows the stop. No fixed-percentage risk. No deposit or capital thresholds.
- [ ] Every calculation redone (Rule #9 convention: 0.01 lot = $0.10 per pip).
- [ ] Event dates and times from the official publisher; Malaysia time correct.
- [ ] Market statements use "tends to" or a labelled view, never certainty.

## Voice

- [ ] Right voice for channel and date.
- [ ] Campaign voice: "aku/kau", short lines, no emoji decoration, trading terms in English.
- [ ] No invented owner trades, results, history or stories.
- [ ] Passes the non-generic test.

## Class and audience

- [ ] Class is Educational or Community invitation.
- [ ] No coaching, broker, account, deposit or referral call to action.
- [ ] Segment declared; SECONDARY pieces carry no recovery framing, deposit mention or urgency.
- [ ] Disclaimer present.

## Series fit

- [ ] Rule number matches the date (31 Oct + N − 1).
- [ ] Follows the current week's slot pattern.
- [ ] Does not repeat a rule already published or queued.

## Process

- [ ] Saved in `CONTENT/<channel>/` with the standard header; status `DRAFT`; not scheduled.
