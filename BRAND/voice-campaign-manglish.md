# Campaign Voice — Manglish (60 Rules)

**Version:** 1.1 · 2026-11-18 (disclosure decision)
**Scope:** the 60 Rules campaign on Threads `themarketdoctrine88`, until the campaign ends on 29 Dec 2026. Institutional TMD content follows `BRAND/tone-of-voice.md`; this file does not change it.
**Basis:** the voice as published on the account between 30 Oct and 18 Nov 2026.

## Who is speaking

- **"Aku"** is the owner. **"Kau"** is the reader.
- A trader who has been through it, talking straight to one person. Firm, never insulting.
- Never invent the owner's trades, results, history or stories. Use "kau" scenes, or real material the owner supplies.

## Language

- Malay base, with trading terms in English: SL, TP, BE, lot, entry, setup, breakout, session, spread, journal, R.
- Everyday connectors: je, tu, dah, takde, kat, nak, macam, sebab, lepas, baru.
- Short lines. One thought per line. White space between ideas.
- No emoji. No hashtags in the post body.

## Signature moves (from published posts)

| Move | Published example |
|---|---|
| Reframe in two short sentences | "Leverage ubah margin. Bukan risk." |
| Name the real cause | "Kau kalah bukan sebab analysis. Kau kalah sebab jam." |
| Short, quotable rule | "Ukur dalam R. Bukan RM." |
| Consequence that lands | "Market ada lagi esok. Akaun kau belum tentu." |
| Position statement | "Aku tak jual harapan. Aku ajar condition." |
| Closing question that invites a reply | "Lot kau semalam: ikut formula, atau ikut feeling?" |

## Structures

**Morning hook (8:30am):** a specific scene with a time or number → what it cost → the reframe → "Tengah hari nanti: Rule #N." (when the rule follows at midday) → a question.

**Rule (12:30pm, or inside the morning post):** the rule in one line → why → "Cara buat:" bullets → a closing contrast line → `Rule #N/60`.

**Evening (9pm):** what others don't say, a pre-release briefing, or an invitation to the free community.

**Sunday (5pm):** the webinar post. The webinar is at 9pm.

## Calls to action

- Free community, PDF and webinar: `WhatsApp aku (link kat bio), type DOCTRINE.` Optionally: `Aku hantar link community.`
- Never write that the link depends on answering questions ("Jawab 3 soalan, aku bagi link"). The questions are optional (owner decision, 18 Nov).
- Coaching (`COACHING`): **on hold** since 18 Nov 2026. Do not use.
- No broker, account, deposit or referral calls to action (`AGENTS/_shared/guardrails.md` §6).

## Never

- Hype, guarantees, "confirm naik", profit screenshots.
- Shaming the reader.
- Fixed-percentage risk rules or deposit thresholds (`AGENTS/_shared/guardrails.md` §4).
- M1 entries. Entry confirmation is M5.
- Any brand name other than THE MARKET DOCTRINE.

## Length

500 characters maximum per Threads post, disclosure included. The disclosure and the blank line before it take 62 characters, leaving 438 for the post itself. Published posts mostly run 300 to 470 characters before the disclosure.

## Disclosure

On **every** published campaign post, including one-line questions and replies (owner decision, 18 Nov 2026; no exception):

> Edukasi je. Bukan nasihat kewangan. Trading ada risiko rugi.

- Last line of the post, after a blank line.
- Provisional wording. It is not proof of regulatory compliance and stays under review.
- Not for paid offers: coaching posts would need a commercial disclosure. Coaching is on hold.
