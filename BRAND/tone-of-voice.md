# Tone of Voice — THE MARKET DOCTRINE

> How TMD sounds. Use this as the checklist before anything is published.

## 1. The voice in one line

A senior analyst explaining the market to an intelligent friend: natural, confident, concise, never selling.

## 2. Writing rules

1. **Lead with the point.** First line states the observation or the question.
2. **One idea per post.** If it needs two ideas, it is two posts.
3. **Short sentences. Plain words.** Technical terms only when they add precision.
4. **Show the reasoning.** "X happened, which matters because Y."
5. **Label certainty.** Separate fact, estimate and opinion.
6. **Include the other side.** State what would prove the view wrong.
7. **End with something useful.** A level to watch, a question to ask, a principle to keep. Not hype.

## 3. Certainty language

| Type | Use phrasing like |
|---|---|
| Fact | "CPI printed at…", "Price closed below…" |
| Estimate | "Roughly…", "On our read…" |
| Opinion | "Our view is…", "We lean…" |
| Scenario | "If X holds, then Y becomes more likely." |
| Invalidation | "This view is wrong if…" |

Never: "will", "guaranteed", "can't lose", "100%", "sure win", "easy money".

## 4. Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Gold rejected the same level for a third session. Buyers are running out of reasons." | "Gold is about to EXPLODE 🚀🚀" |
| "Our bias is lower while price holds under the weekly open. Above it, the idea is invalid." | "Sell now. Trust me." |
| "Most losses come from position size, not direction." | "Follow this secret strategy the banks hide from you." |
| "We don't know. Here is what would tell us." | "Experts agree that…" (unnamed authority) |
| "Educational only. Trading involves risk." | "Join now and start earning daily." |

## 5. Banned patterns

**Hype and false claims**
- Guaranteed or implied returns, income claims, lifestyle bait.
- Fake authority: "institutional secrets", "what banks don't want you to know", unnamed "experts".
- Fake urgency or scarcity.

**Generic AI language** — delete on sight:
- "In today's fast-paced world", "Let's dive in", "Unlock", "Unleash", "Game-changer", "Elevate", "Navigate the landscape", "It's important to note", "In conclusion".
- "It's not just X, it's Y" constructions.
- Rhetorical question openers with no substance ("Ever wondered why…?").
- Lists of three adjectives used for rhythm instead of meaning.
- Emoji as punctuation. Maximum one per post, usually none.

## 6. Channel adjustments

The voice stays the same. The format changes.

| Channel | Length | Style notes |
|---|---|---|
| Threads | 1–5 short posts | Sharpest single insight. First post must stand alone. |
| Instagram | Carousel 5–8 slides or 1 image + caption | Slide 1 = headline. One line per slide. Caption adds context. |
| TikTok | 20–45 sec script | Hook in first 2 seconds. Spoken, simple, one takeaway. |
| WhatsApp | 3–6 short lines | Direct and personal. Key level or lesson. Long disclaimer on broadcasts. |

## 8. Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] Does the first line carry the point?
- [ ] Is every factual claim true and sourced?
- [ ] Are opinions labelled as opinions?
- [ ] Is there an invalidation or risk note where a market view is given?
- [ ] No guaranteed returns, no invented credentials, no fake urgency?
- [ ] No banned AI phrases?
- [ ] Disclaimer included?
- [ ] Would a serious market professional respect this?
