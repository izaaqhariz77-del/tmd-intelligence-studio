*Weekly note — Gold*

Gold fell about 4.5% this week, its worst week since June, closing near $3,885.

The cause was a hot US inflation print. Core CPI came in at 0.3% against 0.2% expected, and markets moved to price a Fed rate hike in December. Yields jumped, the 10-year went above 5%, and the dollar reached its highest since April 2025.

The lesson: gold didn't fall because of inflation. It fell because of what inflation does to interest rates. Gold pays no yield, so when rates rise, it costs more to hold.

Next week: US retail sales (Tue) and Fed minutes (Wed).
Our read: pressure remains below roughly $3,900. We're wrong above $3,903–3,934. Level below is Thursday's low near $3,852.
