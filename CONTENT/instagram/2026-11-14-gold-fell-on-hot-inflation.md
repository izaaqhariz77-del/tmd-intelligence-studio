## Caption

> Gold fell about 4.5% this week, its worst since June, in the same week US inflation came in hotter than expected.
>
> That only looks like a contradiction. A hot print with a Fed already leaning toward tightening pushed markets to price a December hike. Yields jumped, the dollar reached its highest since April 2025, and gold, which pays no interest, became more expensive to hold.
>
> Our read: pressure remains while price holds below roughly $3,900. We're wrong if it reclaims the $3,903–3,934 area. Retail sales on Tuesday and the Fed minutes on Wednesday are the next tests.
>
