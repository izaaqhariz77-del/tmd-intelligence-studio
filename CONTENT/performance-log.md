## What to record

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Views / reach | Did the platform distribute it? |
| Likes | Weak signal of approval |
| Replies / comments | Did it start a conversation? |
| Reposts / shares / saves | Strongest signal: worth passing on or keeping |
| Follows gained | Did it earn an audience? |
| Profile visits / link clicks | Only where the platform shows them |

Do not record or quote numbers you cannot see on the platform.
