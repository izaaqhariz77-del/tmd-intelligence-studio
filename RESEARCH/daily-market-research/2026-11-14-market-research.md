## 3. Why it may have happened (interpretation)

| Driver | Explanation | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Rate repricing after CPI | A hot core print with a Fed already split toward hiking moved the market to price tightening. Higher expected rates raise the return on cash and bonds, which gold does not pay. | High |
| Dollar strength | Gold is priced in dollars. A dollar at its highest since April 2025 makes gold more expensive for non-dollar buyers. | High |
| Front-end yields leading | The 2-year rose more than the 10-year. That pattern points to a policy-rate repricing rather than a long-term inflation scare, which is the less friendly mix for gold. | Medium |
| Positioning / stops | A break to a 13-month low can trigger forced selling. Plausible, but we have no positioning data to support it. | Low |
| PPI relief bounce | The softer monthly PPI gave a brief lift on Friday, but the high annual rate limited it and price faded back under $3,900. | Medium |
